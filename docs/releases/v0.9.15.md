# m365crawl v0.9.15

**Inspect the native evidence behind cached Outlook attendees, without treating
it as a current roster.**

## Native attendee evidence

`calendar attendees sync` explicitly captures native Outlook attendee evidence.
The strictly offline `sources`, `list` and `show` commands expose immutable
physical observations separately from selected-detail bindings, preserving
source-local ambiguity, original loss diagnostics and observation clocks.
Bounded previews include selected-archive routes to the full record and its
own capture, including historical evidence.

These observations do not replace the retained/unioned displayed attendee
roster, resolve a person or establish current meeting membership. Native Mac
HxStore capture and supported copied profiles have separate source scopes;
this release does not qualify native Windows or OWA attendee acquisition.
An exceptional removed-binding retention label remains a known limitation:
do not infer current association from that label alone.

## Capture and recovery

Calendar, native-attendee and people readers share a private-copy lifecycle
and close before verified cleanup. Optional failures stay lane-local;
cleanup or cancellation prevents optional positive admission.

Standalone calendar/native capture preserves pending mail work for the next
eligible ordinary sync. It cannot claim an existing unknown calendar origin:
the refusal precedes calendar, mail and native changes and provides the
ordinary-bootstrap recovery route. Source-ID retries retain a separately
audited admitted original root without guessing selectors or rewriting history.

The archive advances additively from schema 18 to 19. Keep a backup before
upgrading an archive; older readers refuse the newer schema.

## Install and platform scope

On macOS, use `brew install ourostack/tap/m365crawl` or
`brew upgrade m365crawl`, or verify the matching Darwin tarball against
`checksums.txt`. Darwin binaries are Developer ID signed and notarized.

On Windows, download the matching amd64 or arm64 ZIP, verify its checksum,
unzip it and run `m365crawl.exe` from your user-owned installation.
**Windows binaries are intentionally unsigned.**
